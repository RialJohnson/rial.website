A collection of websites I find useful, grouped by topic. I'll add to this as I find more.

Gaming

Backloggery

Where I track games I own, play, and beat. Useful for keeping a backlog from getting completely out of hand.

HowLongToBeat

Crowd-sourced estimates for how long a game takes to finish, from main story to 100%. Helpful when deciding what to play next.

IsThereAnyDeal

Compares PC game prices across stores and tracks historical lows. Good for waiting out a sale instead of guessing.

DoesItPlay?

Tests whether games actually work offline and how much they depend on downloads, accounts, or always-online DRM.

noclip

A browser-based 3D explorer for game maps and levels. Fun for poking around worlds without loading the game itself.

Utilities

Regex101

Interactive regular expression tester with explanations. Saves a lot of trial and error when a pattern is being stubborn.

Wayback Machine

Snapshots of websites over time. Handy when a page disappears or you need to see how something used to look.

Have I Been Pwned

Checks whether an email or password has shown up in known data breaches. Worth running after any leak news.

Misc

I Wanna Buy a House

An interactive map of US cities colored by whether a typical home is in reach of your budget, down payment, and debts.

Flight Atlas

Interactive map of direct flight routes between US airports. Useful for seeing where you can actually fly without a connection.
