A collection of things other people made that I find cool and/or useful.

Gaming

Backloggery

Where I track my backlog and ensure I'm actually playing the games I own. It's a small independent project that I've used for many years.

HowLongToBeat

Crowd-sourced estimates for how long a game takes to finish.

IsThereAnyDeal

Compares PC game prices across stores and tracks historical lows.

DoesItPlay?

Tests whether games actually work offline and how much they depend on downloads, accounts, or always-online DRM.

noclip

A browser-based 3D explorer for game maps and levels. Super useful for capturing screenshots and footage of game levels.

Utilities

Regex101

Interactive regular expression tester with explanations.

Wayback Machine

A complete preservation of the internet over time.

Have I Been Pwned

Checks whether an email or password has shown up in known data breaches.

Misc

I Wanna Buy a House

An interactive map of US cities colored by whether a typical home is in reach of your budget.

Flight Atlas

Interactive map of direct flight routes between US airports.
